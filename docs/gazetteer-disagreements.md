# Gazetteer spelling list: readings that disagree

Source: *Gazetteer of the Union Territory Goa, Daman and Diu, Part 1* (1979), "A list of spellings of place names, Goa District", pages 997 to 1007 (https://archive.org/details/dli.ministry.08183, scan leaves 1013 to 1023).

Every line of the list was read twice: once from the page image by Claude, once by OCR (Tesseract 5.5, Marathi and Hindi data for the Devanagari column, English for the rest). A value went into `data/village_names_review.xlsx` only where both readings are exactly the same. Every reading, kept or not, is in `data/gazetteer/readings_1979.csv`.

- Lines in the list: 402. Matched to an LGD village: 300. Villages given at least one Gazetteer value: 298.
- Values kept: Government spelling 286, Survey of India spelling 140, Advisory Body spelling 269, Devanagari 103.
- Lines with at least one disagreement: 287. Those cells are empty in the review sheet, waiting for a reviewer.
- Aldona (626704) was transcribed by hand before this pass and was left exactly as it was.

## How to settle one
Open the crop, read the line, and type the value into the matching column of `data/village_names_review.xlsx` (the row with that LGD code). "unsure" means Claude could not read it with confidence; "(blank)" means no text was read there (in the Survey of India column, "...." on the page means the Survey of India gives no spelling). The Devanagari is the Gazetteer's own 1970 spelling: it shows only on the village sheet, marked "Needs review", never on the map (phase 2, rule 6).
