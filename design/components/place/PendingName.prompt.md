Shown where a Konkani (Devanagari or Romi) name should be but is not recorded yet. Villages, and Dharbandora.
```jsx
<PendingName ink="var(--house-neel-on)" />
<PendingName label="Two spellings in use" />
```
- The shimmer is a 4.2s opacity breathe (`khoim-shell` in tokens/elements.css). It stops under reduced motion. The label always sits on a raised tile, so it stays AAA.
